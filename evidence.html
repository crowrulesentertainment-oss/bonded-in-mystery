THE EVIDENCE ROOM

A good investigation makes it clear what we know, what someone claims and what remains a theory.

VERIFIED FACT

Confirmed information

Supported by reliable, attributable sources or direct documentation.

PHYSICAL EVIDENCE

Material records

Objects, photographs, recordings or documents with provenance and context.

WITNESS ACCOUNT

First-hand report

A person's account, presented with attribution and without treating it as independently confirmed.

UNVERIFIED CLAIM

Not yet confirmed

A statement that needs corroboration before it can be treated as established.

THEORY

Possible explanation

An interpretation that may fit some facts but is not itself proof.

DEBUNKED / DISPUTED

Contradicted information

Claims that reliable evidence has disproved or materially challenged.

OUR STANDARD

We do not identify private individuals as suspects, repeat unsupported accusations or present speculation about real crimes as fact. Sources and corrections belong with each published case.
