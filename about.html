OUR MISSION

CURIOUS MINDS.
OPEN CASES.

Bonded in Mystery is a husband-and-wife mystery show hosted by Jon and Jen. We investigate unsolved stories, paranormal claims, strange history, UFOs and anything that makes us ask: what really happened?

OUR PROMISE

We bring curiosity without pretending to have every answer. We research together, alternate episode leads, challenge each other's ideas and make room for humor alongside serious investigation.

We distinguish verified facts, physical evidence, witness accounts, unverified claims and theories. We cite sources, correct mistakes and avoid turning speculation into accusations — especially when real people and unresolved crimes are involved.

ONE INVESTIGATION. TWO PERSPECTIVES.

Some mysteries may be solved. Some may be explained. Others may remain unknown. We believe being honest about the difference is part of the story.
