A short practice challenge. Read the facts carefully, then decide what the evidence actually supports.

TRAINING SCENARIO / FICTIONAL

THE LIGHT IN THE EMPTY CABIN

A park ranger records a light in a remote cabin at 11:40 PM. The cabin is listed as closed for the season. The next morning, the ranger finds no footprints near the door. The camera recording is blurry and shows only a bright rectangle in a window.

WHAT CAN WE CONCLUDE?

The point of an investigation is to distinguish what was observed from what we assume it means.
